## Things that changed while getting green (all in DECISIONS.md)

1. **Δt convention.** The first generator draft indexed month lengths one step off the time axis, leaving 0.5 % residuals. Fixed: Δt_n = t_n − t_{n−1} everywhere.
2. **pywaterflood first-sample convention.** pywaterflood adds an injection response at t_0 on top of its primary term, so the exact CRMP truth could only be recovered to 10.4 % on the smallest pair. Zeroing i(0) in the arrays passed to pywaterflood makes the two models identical; recovery is then exact. The in-house solver (M1) will use the Sayarpour convention directly.
3. **Aquifer signature.** Corrected the meaning of "Σf_ij > 1.15": it is the unconstrained per-injector sum / field support ratio, not the per-producer sum (P-3 in the streak case legitimately receives 1.59).
4. **Sector O_d.** `sectored_60` fails O_d as a whole field and passes per sector, so sectorization is a requirement for that case.
5. **Python 3.11 instead of 3.12**, **Node.js absent** (needed by Milestone 4), and **CRM-forward rather than finite-difference generator** — all recorded with reasons.
